feat(xtgls): implement corr(ar1) from the ado source; it lands at machine precision

The entry was an E1 capability gap -- `xtgls, corr(ar1)` raised UnsupportedError. It now estimates, and the
independent sweep grades it A: coefficients 5.40e-16, standard errors 3.42e-16 against a live Stata.

THE ALGORITHM, read from `xtgls.ado` program `mc1` (lines 759-815) and `_crcar1.ado`'s `regress` branch:

  1. OLS on the raw data, residual e.
  2. Per panel, the least-squares AR(1) coefficient
         rho_i = sum(e_t e_{t-1}) / sum(e_{t-1}^2)
     pooled into ONE common rho weighted by panel SIZE:
         rho = sum_i rho_i N_i / sum_i N_i
  3. Prais-Winsten transform every variable, per panel:
         first observation  q = sqrt(1-rho^2) x
         later observations q = x_t - rho x_{t-1}
  4. The existing GLS accumulation on the TRANSFORMED data.

Three details are easy to get wrong and all are taken from the source: rho is pooled by panel size rather
than averaged equally; the transform is Prais-Winsten (the first observation is scaled and kept, not
dropped as Cochrane-Orcutt would); and the transform happens BEFORE the GLS weighting, so the estimator is
unchanged and the data is what differs. That last point is why the previous rejection was correct -- it just
was not the end of the story.

corr(psar1) still raises, now with a message that explains why: `mc2` estimates a separate rho per panel,
which gives Omega a different block structure, so returning the common-rho answer under that label would be a
wrong estimate wearing a right name.
