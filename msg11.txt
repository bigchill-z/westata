`reg3.ado` line 533 delegates `vce(robust)` to `_reg3robust`, whose GetSandwich builds a standard sandwich:

    psi_t[i] = x_i(t) * sum_j precision[i,j] * e_j(t)
    V        = (X Omega^-1 X)^-1 [ sum_t psi psi ] (X Omega^-1 X)^-1
