fix(heckman): take the selection block's covariance from the probit, and use the observed information

Two defects in the same file, both found by measuring rather than reading, both confirmed by the
independent sweep afterwards.

1. `_probit_fit` REPORTED THE EXPECTED INFORMATION; STATS REPORTS THE OBSERVED ONE.

For a probit the expected information is Z' diag(pdf^2/(Phi(1-Phi))) Z. That is what the function used,
and it is correct only asymptotically. Stata's `probit` reports the observed information:

    expected  se [0.06449711, 0.07793239, 0.06549953]   max rel 7.373e-03
    observed  se [0.06424488, 0.07851124, 0.06579817]   max rel 1.770e-11
    Stata     se [0.06424488, 0.07851124, 0.06579817]

The 0.4-0.7% error, in different directions per parameter, propagated into every two-step selection
covariance in the package.

An analytic second derivative of the probit score was attempted FIRST and was wrong by 6.3% -- worse
than the approximation it replaced. So the observed information is differenced from the verified
closed-form score instead. That is the second time in this session that a correct first derivative did
not survive being hand-differentiated; the numerical route is used deliberately, not lazily.

2. THE SELECTION BLOCK CAME FROM THE JOINT SANDWICH; STATS LEAVES THE FIRST STAGE ALONE.

Stata's two-step heckman does not touch the first stage's inference, because the selection equation was
estimated on its own. Measured:

    z1     heckman 0.0785112048   probit 0.0785112404   ratio 0.99999955
    z2     heckman 0.0657981521   probit 0.0657981707   ratio 0.99999972
    _cons  heckman 0.0642448607   probit 0.0642448773   ratio 0.99999974

westata used the joint sandwich's block, which put select:z1 3.95% away and select:z2 1.41% away in
opposite directions. Taking the probit block:

    select:z1    3.948e-02 -> 4.531e-07
    select:z2    1.414e-02 -> 2.826e-07
    select:_cons 3.688e-02 -> 2.574e-07

and the coefficients were already at 4.878e-13.

Independent confirmation from re-running the domain against a live Stata:
`heckman twostep` se_rel 6.81e-02 -> 3.06e-02.

STILL OPEN, and documented rather than papered over: the outcome block and lambda remain 1.6% to 5.9%
off. `_two_step_covariance` builds an M-estimator sandwich; Stata reports Heckman's two-step corrected
VCE, which is a different quantity. The correction was pursued by fitting candidate structures to Stata's
own V -- the closest, `(A'A)^-1 A' diag(dlambda/dw) A (A'A)^-1`, still left a 6.3e-3 residual, and its
fitted scalar grew with rho in a way `rho^2` does not explain, so the structure is not yet right and no
formula was adopted on a hunch. That is why the gap is 3.06e-02 instead of closed.
