`e(V)[0,0]` in Stata is the variance of x1, because Stata puts `_cons` LAST. `cov_params()[0,0]` here is the
INTERCEPT, because the intercept comes first. Comparing them produced "our variance is 1.83x too small", and each
of four rounds then explained that non-existent discrepancy in turn: fweight semantics (wrong), RNG consumption
(wrong -- the uniform before and after qreg is byte-identical), sort persistence (real, but not this), LP vertex
choice (wrong).

The per-replicate work was not wasted: weights match Stata element for element, the fit matches to 3.5e-16, and the
carried permutation genuinely helps (0.052543 with it, 0.049441 without, against Stata's 0.054044). But the
magnitude that motivated it was an artifact.

The remaining VCE difference is Monte-Carlo: Stata's OWN value moves 42% between rep counts for this estimator
(0.037930 at reps=20, 0.054044 at reps=400), so neither side has converged and a pointwise standard is not
well-defined. 8.18 now records that rather than a "3.9e-02 gap".
