fix(heckman, etregress): read the corrected VCE out of the ado source instead of fitting it

Round 3 ended with heckman's outcome block 5.9% off and no formula: five candidate structures were fitted
numerically, the best left a 6.3e-03 residual, and its fitted scalar grew with rho in a way rho^2 could not
explain. The conclusion then was that the structure was not yet right and no formula would be adopted on a
hunch. This round read heckman.ado.

`Heck2` (lines 858-897) gives it exactly:

    XpX1    = (A'A)^-1                        A = [X, _cons, lambda] on the selected rows
    delthat = lambda (lambda + w)
    F       = sum_i delthat_i A[i,:]' Z[i,:]  the SELECTION regressors
    Q       = rho^2 F Vprb F'                 Vprb = the PROBIT's covariance
    VBm     = sum_i (1 - rho^2 delthat_i) A[i,:]' A[i,:]
    V       = sigma^2 XpX1 (VBm + Q) XpX1

Why the numerical fit missed it, and the two things it got wrong:

  * F is a WEIGHTED cross-product with weight delthat, not the unweighted A' diag(dlambda) A that fitted
    best. The fit was chasing the same matrix through the wrong parameterisation, which is why no scalar
    worked.
  * Q is scaled by rho^2 and carries the probit covariance through F, rather than a scalar fitted to the
    data. That explains the symptom that defeated the fit: the scalar appeared to grow with rho faster than
    rho^2 because Vprb and the weighted cross-product were missing from the expression.

etregress.ado `TwoStep` (lines 501-534) applies the same correction with one difference that matters: its
`mtao` is the mean of tao over the FULL sample, because the treatment equation is estimated there, while
heckman averages over the SELECTED rows only. Both are now implemented, and both take the selection block
from the probit -- Stata leaves the first stage's inference untouched.

Measured against a live Stata, then confirmed by re-running the domain:

    etregress twostep   se 6.77e-02 -> 2.174e-11      (3.1e9 times better, machine precision)
    heckman   twostep   se 6.81e-02 -> 1.880e-05      (3600 times better)

heckman's residual 1.9e-05 is not the formula: it is how the two `_cons` entries align, since this package
names them `y:Intercept` / `select:Intercept` while the sweep compares positionally. So heckman stays E2 and
etregress moves to E3 -- on the measurement, not on the change.

Also removed: `_two_step_covariance`, the M-estimator sandwich both models used before. It is dead code
now, and worse than dead -- its docstring claims to own inference for these models, which is known to be
wrong. iv.py has its own `_two_step_covariance` for IV/GMM, a different quantity, untouched.

The sweep caught an error worth recording: after editing the registry to E3 I ran it before re-aggregating,
and it failed with "etregress declared E3, measured worst E" against the stale aggregate. That is the check
working exactly as designed -- a level edited on the strength of a belief rather than a measurement is
caught even when the belief happens to be right.

Suite: 1408 passing. This is the fourth time this project has been rescued by reading the ado source
instead of inferring behaviour from output.
