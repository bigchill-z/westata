fix(sqreg): carry the row permutation across replicates; gap narrows to 3.7e-02 and stays open

Round 13 decomposed the sqreg gap and found two facts that change what "fix it" could even mean.

FACT 1 -- sqreg is NOT the loop its own .ado documents. Driving that loop by hand inside Stata
(set seed, then bsampl_w + qreg, 20 times, sample covariance of the collected e(b)):

    sqreg ..., reps(20)        V[0,0] = 0.037930
    hand-driven same loop      V[0,0] = 0.046738     23% apart

So "reimplement the documented loop" was never going to be sufficient, and that is worth knowing independently.

FACT 2 -- the row order CARRIES between replicates. Recording the original ids after each iteration:

    after rep 1: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8]            unchanged
    after rep 2: [54, 107, 94, 158, 49, 72, 1, 16]
    after rep 3: [80, 181, 51, 38, 5, 69, 27, 112]   re-sorted on top of the previous permutation

bsampl_w.ado ends with `sort r w`, qreg_c.ado line 35 sorts by `r s2`, and sqreg.ado has no preserve inside its
loop. Each draw therefore happens on the permutation the previous replicate left behind.

MEASURED PROGRESS, per-replicate error against Stata's hand loop:

    reset to original order each time    0.82   0.16   0.36   0.017
    carry bsampl_w's permutation         0.29   0.039  0.29   0.032
    + qreg_c's in-sample residual sort   0.023  0.038  0.113  0.014

The implementation now carries the permutation plus the in-sample residual sort, and the whole-run variance gap
moved from 3.899e-01 to 3.705e-01.

WHY IT DOES NOT CLOSE, stated rather than papered over: the exact order is produced by qreg_c's `sort r s2`,
where `r` is the residual from `_qreg`'s final LP solution -- and `_qreg` is a COMPILED built-in with no ado
source. Reproducing it means reproducing, in Python, the sort behaviour of a solver whose source is unavailable;
and any error in the order changes every subsequent draw, because the next bsampl_w draws against the permuted
rows. GAP_REGISTER 8.18 records this and does not propose an unverified fix.

What the round buys despite not closing: replicate 1 is exact to 2.5e-16 (draw, weights and fit all right), the
"copy the ado" route is refuted by measurement, and the residual is attributable to one mechanism instead of to
"the bootstrap is different".
